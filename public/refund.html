Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

In short: confirmed tickets can't be cancelled by you. If the event is cancelled, you get a full refund automatically. If money is deducted but no ticket is issued, it is refunded automatically within 5–7 working days.

1. Cancellation by you

Once a booking is confirmed and the QR ticket is issued, it cannot be cancelled, changed or refunded — including if you cannot attend, arrive late, or are refused entry for breaking the event or venue rules.

2. If the event is cancelled

If the organiser cancels the event, you will receive a full refund of the amount paid to the original payment method. You do not need to apply — we will inform you by SMS / WhatsApp and start the refund within 7 working days of the cancellation.

3. If the event is postponed or the venue changes

Your ticket stays valid for the new date or venue. If you cannot attend the new date, you may request a refund within 7 days of the change being announced, using the contact details below. Requests after that period cannot be accepted.

4. Payment deducted but no ticket

If money was deducted from your account but your booking was not confirmed (for example, due to a network or bank issue), the payment is automatically reversed by our payment partner, usually within 5–7 working days. If you were charged twice for the same booking, the extra payment is refunded in the same way. You can check a booking's status under My tickets.

5. How refunds are paid

  • Refunds are always paid to the original payment method (card, UPI, net banking or wallet) — we cannot pay to a different account or in cash.
  • After we start a refund, it normally reaches your account within 5–7 working days, depending on your bank.
  • Payment gateway or convenience charges, if any were shown at checkout, are refunded only when the event is cancelled.

6. Ticket transfers

Tickets are issued in the names given at booking and cannot be transferred to other people after booking, unless the organiser allows it.
