Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

In short: we collect only what is needed to book your tickets and let you into the event. We never sell your data, we never see your card or UPI details, and you can ask us to delete your account at any time.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use this website to discover events and book tickets, how we use it, and the choices you have. It is written to comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 of India.

2. Information we collect

  • Mobile number — to sign you in with a one-time password (OTP) and to send your tickets.
  • Booking details of the main contact — name, email address, postal address, age and (optionally) gender.
  • Details of each attendee — name, age, and optionally gender and mobile number, so that each person on the booking can be admitted.
  • Booking and payment records — events booked, ticket categories, amount paid, payment status and the reference number from our payment partner.
  • Entry records — the time each attendee's ticket is scanned at the venue.
  • Technical information — IP address and basic browser information, used for security, fraud prevention and preventing abuse (for example, limiting OTP requests).

We do not collect or store your card number, CVV, UPI PIN or net-banking password. Payments are processed directly by our payment partners.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your bookings and generate your QR ticket.
  • To send your ticket and booking confirmation by SMS and WhatsApp, and to send important updates about your event (such as a change of time or venue, or a cancellation).
  • To verify tickets and admit attendees at the venue.
  • To process payments and refunds, and to keep records required by law (for example, tax and accounting records).
  • To pre-fill your details the next time you book.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent fraud and misuse.

We will not send you promotional messages without your consent.

4. Who we share it with

We share your information only with the parties needed to provide the service:

  • Payment partners (such as Razorpay or Cashfree) — to process your payment and any refund.
  • Messaging providers — our SMS gateway and WhatsApp Business service provider, to deliver OTPs, tickets and event updates.
  • The event organiser and venue staff — attendee names and ticket details, for entry and event management.
  • Hosting provider — the servers on which this website and its data are stored.
  • Authorities — when required by law, court order or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the public.

We never sell or rent your personal information.

5. Cookies

We use only essential cookies that keep you signed in and keep your booking secure. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

6. How long we keep it

We keep booking and payment records for as long as required under applicable tax and accounting laws. Your account details are kept while your account is active. Sign-in codes (OTPs) expire within minutes, and expired sessions are deleted.

7. How we protect it

Your data is transferred over encrypted (HTTPS) connections, access to the admin area is restricted to authorised staff, and sign-in codes and sessions are stored in hashed form. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or withdraw your consent, by contacting us using the details below. Some records (such as payment records) may need to be kept for legal reasons even after you ask for deletion.

9. Children

Bookings must be made by a person aged 18 or above. Details of minors attending an event should be provided by their parent or guardian.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.
